DEEP NEURAL NETWORKS (DNN) - SEMESTER SYLLABUS
Academic Year: 2025-26 | Version: v1 | Approved: August 2025

UNIT 1: Foundations of Neural Networks
Biological inspiration, perceptron model, multi-layer perceptrons,
activation functions (sigmoid, tanh, ReLU, softmax), forward propagation.

UNIT 2: Training Neural Networks
Loss functions, gradient descent, backpropagation algorithm, learning rate,
optimizers (SGD, Momentum, Adam, RMSProp), overfitting, regularization,
dropout, batch normalization.

UNIT 3: Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN)
Convolution operation, pooling layers, CNN architectures (LeNet, AlexNet,
VGG, ResNet), applications in image classification.

UNIT 4: Recurrent Neural Networks (RNN)
Sequence modeling, RNN, vanishing gradient problem, Long Short-Term Memory
(LSTM), Gated Recurrent Units (GRU), applications in NLP and time series.

UNIT 5: Advanced Topics
Autoencoders, transfer learning, attention mechanism, introduction to
transformer architecture.
