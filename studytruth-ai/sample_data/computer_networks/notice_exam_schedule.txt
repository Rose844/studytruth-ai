Subject: End Semester Theory Examination Schedule — Computer Networks (CN)

All students registered for CN (Semester 5) are informed that the End
Semester Theory Examination for Computer Networks will be conducted as
per the following schedule:

Exam Date: 2026-10-06
Reporting Time: 09:00 AM
Exam Duration: 3 hours
Venue: Examination Hall Block-C
Maximum Marks: 100 (Theory: 80, Internal Assessment: 20)

Instructions to students:
1. Students must carry their college ID card and hall ticket.
2. The syllabus for this examination is the revised 2025-26 syllabus
   (version v2), which includes the updated Unit 3 covering VLAN and

3. Electronic devices, including calculators with programmable memory,
   are not permitted inside the examination hall.
4. Students are advised to reach the venue at least 30 minutes before
   the reporting time.
